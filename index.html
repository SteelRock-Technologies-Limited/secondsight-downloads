First time on a device

  1. On the device: Settings › About phone / About tablet › Software information, tap Build number seven times. Then Settings › Developer options › USB debugging on.
  2. Plug the device into the computer with a data cable and tap Allow on the USB-debugging prompt.
  3. Unzip the download and run SecondSight Updater (on a Mac, right-click "SecondSight Updater.command" and choose Open the first time). Put your name in and press UPDATE DEVICE. It checks the ATAK on the device, installs the plugin and keeps your SecondSight settings.
  4. Start ATAK on the device. SecondSight is under Tools › Plugins, and signs in with the account you were given.
